Privacy Policy

Controller: Malte Hombergs, Email: malte@hombergs.net

Service: OGC JSON Proxy available at https://vpn.hombergs.top/ (the “Service”). The Service converts OGC WFS/WMS XML responses from public geospatial endpoints into JSON/GeoJSON for use by client applications, including ChatGPT Actions.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we process when you use the Service, for what purposes and on which legal bases (Art. 6 GDPR), how long we keep data, to whom we may disclose it, and which rights you have as a data subject. It applies to requests made to vpn.hombergs.top and the minimal website routes (e.g., /privacy, /terms). It does not cover the third-party OGC endpoints you choose (e.g., Geoportal Berlin) — their policies also apply when you send requests to them via this proxy.

2. Summary (Plain Language)

  • We do not build user profiles and do not sell personal data.
  • We keep only standard web-server logs (IP address, timestamp, user agent, request path) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Your OGC query parameters (e.g., endpoint, typenames, bbox) are forwarded to the OGC service you select; they are not stored by us beyond transient processing in RAM.
  • Log retention is typically up to 14 days, then automatic deletion/rotation.
  • You can contact malte@hombergs.net to exercise your GDPR rights.

3. Categories of Data

3.1 Technical connection data (server logs)

  • IP address, date/time of access, request method and path, HTTP status code, response size, user agent, referrer (if provided).

Purpose: operate and secure the Service, detect abuse (e.g., denial-of-service), diagnose errors, and produce minimal operational metrics.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).

3.2 Request content you submit

When you call the API (e.g., /ogc/feature), you send parameters such as endpoint, service, typenames, bbox, srsname, filter. These are required to fulfill your request and are forwarded to the target OGC server you designated.

  • We do not persist these parameters or the returned geodata, except transiently in memory and the minimal traces present in standard access/error logs (see 3.1).

Purpose: fulfill your request (proxying/conversion).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract-like usage of the Service) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (operation/security).

4. Retention

  • Access/error logs: routinely rotated and deleted after up to 14 days, unless a longer retention is necessary to investigate concrete security incidents or abuse.
  • Transient processing: request payloads and responses are held in RAM only for the time needed to proxy and deliver the result.

5. Recipients and International Transfers

  • OGC endpoints you select: We forward your request parameters to the third-party OGC service (e.g., Geoportal Berlin). That third party becomes an independent controller of the data it receives; their own privacy policy applies.
  • Infrastructure providers: The Service is hosted on a VPS in the EU/EEA (or an equivalent jurisdiction). Standard server administration tools may process log data for reliability and security.

We do not otherwise disclose personal data to third parties except when required by applicable law or to enforce our terms or defend legal claims. We do not intentionally transfer personal data outside the EEA; if a transfer becomes necessary, we will rely on an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR) or appropriate safeguards (Art. 46 GDPR), such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies or third-party trackers for the API or the minimal policy pages. Analytics scripts are not embedded.

7. Automated Decision-Making / Profiling

No automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR is performed.

8. Security

  • Transport security via HTTPS (TLS) for all external endpoints.
  • Reverse proxy isolation and firewall rules to limit inbound/outbound access.
  • Log minimization and scheduled rotation.
  • Principle of least privilege for system services.

While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures, no internet service can be 100% secure. Please avoid including unnecessary personal data in OGC parameters.

9. Your Rights (GDPR)

Subject to the legal conditions, you have the following rights: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence or where an alleged infringement took place.

To exercise your rights, contact: malte@hombergs.net. For log-based data, please include the approximate time and your IP address to help us identify relevant entries.

10. Legal Bases in Detail

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – processing necessary to provide the Service you request (proxying and converting your calls).
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interests in operating a secure, reliable service; preventing abuse; diagnosing faults.
  • If we are legally obliged to retain or disclose certain data, we rely on Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).

11. Children’s Data

The Service is intended for technical users and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process children’s personal data.

12. Third-Party Policies

When you select an OGC endpoint (e.g., a WFS/WMS server run by a public body), your request parameters are sent to that server. Their privacy policy governs their processing. Please consult their documentation before use. If you do not agree with a third party’s processing, do not route requests to that endpoint via this Service.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. The latest version is always available at https://vpn.hombergs.top/privacy. Material changes will be indicated by updating the “Last updated” date below.

14. Contact

Questions or requests regarding data protection can be sent to: malte@hombergs.net.
